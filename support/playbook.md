# Support Playbook — Evergreen Academy

Seed entries for Piper, the support desk. Format ported from pab-support-agent
(github.com/heymitch/pab-support-agent, MIT). **Replace these with real past
replies** — a real answer ("the download is under Course Resources") is what
makes a draft worth approving. When no entry matches, Piper says so and
escalates instead of inventing policy.

Approved replies compound automatically: each ✅'d send is saved as a learned
entry (taste memory, subject_kind `support_reply`) and grounds future drafts.

---

## Entry 1: Course access after purchase (access)
**Topic:** access
**Question:** I bought a course but can't see it in my account.
**Answer:**
> Thanks for grabbing it! Access is tied to the email you checked out with — log in with that exact address and it'll be on your dashboard. If you used a different email at checkout, tell me which one and I'll move it over.

---

## Entry 2: Where are the downloads / workbook (content)
**Topic:** content
**Question:** Where do I find the workbook and downloads for the course?
**Answer:**
> Every course keeps its downloads in the Course Resources section, right under the last lesson of module 1. The workbook PDF is fillable — open it in a real PDF app (not the browser preview) and your answers save.

---

## Entry 3: Refund request (refund)
**Topic:** refund
**Question:** This isn't the right fit for me. Can I get a refund?
**Answer:**
> Appreciate you giving it an honest shot — that's exactly what the guarantee is for. I'll start the refund today; you'll see it back on your card in 3–5 business days. If you're open to a line on what didn't fit, it genuinely helps me improve the course. Totally optional.

---

## Entry 4: Do existing students get new material (general)
**Topic:** general
**Question:** I already bought — do I get the new lessons/bonuses too?
**Answer:**
> Yep — every update to a course you own lands in your account automatically, no action needed. That's the deal: buy once, keep getting the improvements.

---

## Entry 5: Receipt / invoice for taxes (billing)
**Topic:** billing
**Question:** Can I get an invoice or receipt for my purchase?
**Answer:**
> Of course — the payment processor emailed a receipt at purchase (check spam for a stripe.com sender). If you need a proper invoice with your business details on it, reply with the name/address you want on it and I'll send one over.
